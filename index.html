About Friends

Who Friends of Go Global are

Friends are individuals and households who want to walk with us — praying, giving, learning, and sharing in the joy of seeing the kingdom expand among the nations.

Partners in the mission

You care that the unreached hear the Gospel, and you want a clear, trusted way to stand with those who are there for the long haul.

Part of the adventure

Receive updates, join live calls with pastors, and stay close to what God is doing — not as spectators, but as friends who share the journey.

Distinct from church partnership

Churches exploring formal partnership with Go Global follow a different pathway on our main site. Friends is for personal supporters and prayer partners.

Looking for church partnership or training? Visit goglobalfamily.org — Friends of Go Global sits alongside that work as our outward-facing home for individual supporters.

The work

Reaching the unreached with the Gospel

Go Global is an apostolic family of churches. At the heart of our life together is fellowship — shared vision, values, and a commitment to make disciples and plant churches.

We focus on long-term relationships with indigenous pastors and apostolic missionary workers. We multiply disciples, establish churches, release ministries, and strengthen leaders — Word and Spirit, aiming for sustainable churches rather than creating dependency.
